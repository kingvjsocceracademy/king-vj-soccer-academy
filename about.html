ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

Creating a positive place for young people to discover the joy of football and reach their potential.

WHO WE ARE

Football is the game. Growth is the goal.

KING VJ SOCCER ACADEMY is a youth-focused football academy in Uganda. We believe football can help children develop important life skills while building a love for the game.

Our approach puts young players first. We want every child to feel welcome, learn the basics, build confidence and enjoy being part of a team.
