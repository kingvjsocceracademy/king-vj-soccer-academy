Our Programs

Building confident, disciplined, and skilled footballers through age-appropriate coaching, teamwork, and a love for the game.

Train. Learn. Grow.

At KING VJ Soccer Academy, every player gets an opportunity to develop. Our programs focus on football fundamentals, fitness, discipline, teamwork, and match experience in a supportive environment.

FOUNDATION

Junior Development

Fun, structured sessions for young players to learn ball control, passing, coordination, confidence, and the basics of football.

SKILL BUILDING

Technical Training

Focused practice in dribbling, first touch, passing, shooting, movement, and decision-making—with regular feedback from coaches.

TEAMWORK

Team & Tactical Training

Players learn positioning, communication, teamwork, match strategy, and how to work together as a team.

MATCH EXPERIENCE

Games & Competitions

Friendly matches and competitive opportunities help players apply their skills, build resilience, and enjoy the spirit of fair play.

SPECIALIST

Goalkeeper Training

Dedicated goalkeeper work on handling, positioning, footwork, diving technique, distribution, and confidence.

PLAYER GROWTH

Fitness & Character

Age-appropriate fitness alongside lessons in discipline, respect, leadership, commitment, and sportsmanship.
