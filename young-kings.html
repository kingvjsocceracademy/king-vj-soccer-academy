AGES 5–7 YEARS

BIG DREAMS START SMALL

First touches. Big confidence.

The Young Kings program introduces children aged 5–7 to football in a fun, welcoming and encouraging environment. At this age, the most important things are enjoying movement, discovering the ball and feeling proud to learn something new.

Sessions can include playful activities that help children develop coordination, balance, listening, sharing and basic football skills.
